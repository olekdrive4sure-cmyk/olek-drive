Questions

Before you ask

When does Olek Drive launch?
Closed beta in Ikeja, Lekki, VI, Yaba and Ajah begins February 2027, with public Lagos launch in March 2027. Abuja, Port Harcourt and Ibadan follow in 2027; Accra and Abidjan in 2028; Dar es Salaam and Kampala in 2029.
Can I pay cash?
Yes. Cash, card, bank transfer, Olek Wallet, Apple Pay, Google Pay and USSD — pay however works for you. Drivers receive weekly bank payouts regardless of how you paid.
How is Olek different from Bolt?
Capped commissions for drivers (they earn more, they stay), a 24/7 human safety desk, no surge above 1.5×, cash-native payments, works on entry-level Androids and 3G, and a Nigerian company you can actually call. Simple.
How much does Olek Bike cost?
From ₦300 base and ₦120/km — usually 40–60% of a car fare for the same trip. Certified helmet for you and the rider, every ride, every time.
How do drivers join?
Pre-register above. We'll verify your identity (NIN), licence (FRSC), LASRRA card, LASDRI certificate, vehicle (VIO + hackney permit) and insurance — then you're on the road within days, not weeks.
What about my data?
Minimum necessary collection, 90-day location purge, encrypted opt-in audio, and government requests honoured only via lawful process. NDPA 2023 registered. Your data is not our business model — rides are.
